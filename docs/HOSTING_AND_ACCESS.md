## Cold starts and storage — unavoidable free-tier compromises

Render Free is commonly documented to spin down after15 minutes idle and may take about a minute to resume. These figures must be rechecked against current Render docs after network access is enabled; they are **not** an uptime/latency promise. Judge links must warn about the possible warm-up and offer the recorded walkthrough/evidence alongside the app link. A real person opening the app shortly before a scheduled presentation is legitimate; scheduled pings, synthetic keep-alive traffic, cron jobs and uptime bots are **not** part of this setup.

SQLite remains **ephemeral** on this service: restart, deploy, platform replacement or spin-down can lose investigations. Do not rely on any completed session remaining available through November18. New deterministic cases can be recreated; users must export JSON/HTML immediately. The UI states this risk and preserves saved IDs through transient503/network failures; confirmed missing sessions offer a new-case path. The public, independently replayable sample reports in `docs/submission/` survive service filesystem loss because they are versioned in Git. They do not turn SQLite into persistent storage.

Minimize loss/cold-start risk without changing the architecture: freeze the verified branch, disable auto-deploys, export reports, retain offline evidence/recording, check access manually at meaningful milestones, and leave enough warm-up time before judging. Review workspace free-instance hours, build/egress quotas, any trial/verification restrictions, and suspension notices; other services in the account can consume shared quotas. Free availability is **best effort**, not guaranteed uptime. A public URL alone does not prove it will remain reachable through the requested date.
