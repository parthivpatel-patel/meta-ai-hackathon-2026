## Product description

FORGE is an industrial investigation workstation that connects an interactive simulated machine to observed telemetry, competing diagnostic hypotheses, controlled synthetic experiments and independent numerical verification. An operator inspects a component, captures an abnormal observation window, compares explanations, confirms informative tests and reviews supporting or contradictory evidence. Exported JSON/HTML reports reproduce the investigation without rerunning a model. The first application is a simulated motor-driven centrifugal pump; no real equipment is controlled.

## Differentiation and technical contribution

The central contribution is a verifiable investigation loop, not an alert explanation or decorative 3D model. Proposals, experiment policy, simulator and numerical verifier have separate boundaries. Process departures and instrument errors are distinguished with reference/control evidence. Unsupported claims remain contradicted or inconclusive. Every source, observation window, simulated test and limitation is recorded; offline replay detects inconsistent evidence. The machine view is connected to the same instruments/capture context and offers an accessible 2D path.

The prepared Meta contribution is structured hypothesis generation from permitted, timestamped observations through Baseten's exact owner-supplied event model. The workstation can consume those validated proposals while retaining independent test selection and verification. **Current evidence is deterministic/local-fixture evidence, not genuine Meta inference.** Do not submit it as fulfillment of the eligible-model requirement. After a separately authorized genuine run, describe only the actual contribution and measured result; report failures/missing usage honestly. Attribution and the applicable model/Usage Policy requirements must be satisfied.
